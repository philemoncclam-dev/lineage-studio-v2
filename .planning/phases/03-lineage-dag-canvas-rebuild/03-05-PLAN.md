Purpose: This is where DAG-02's core correctness (edges anchor to exact rows via handles) and TRUST-01's provenance channel become real, and where the retired SVG view's hand-rolled `getBoundingClientRect` measurement is replaced by xyflow `<Handle>` primitives. The look is unchanged — only the positioning/rendering mechanism is new.
Output: Two node components, one edge component + its test, and the ported stylesheet.
</objective>
